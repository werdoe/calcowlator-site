Privacy Policy

CalcOwlator is a list calculator for budgets, bills, groceries, goals and anything else you add up. Your lists are yours. This page explains, in plain words, what happens to them.

The short version

  • We don't collect any of your data. There are no accounts to sign up for.
  • Your lists, accounts and settings are stored on your iPhone.
  • If you turn on iCloud sync, they're also kept in your own private iCloud, which only you can read.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no selling or sharing of data.

What the app stores, and where

Lists, rows, amounts and notes, accounts (the names, colours and balances you enter), groups and settings are saved on your device, inside the app's own storage. They never leave your device unless you turn on iCloud sync or make a backup yourself. Like most apps' data, they are included in your iPhone's own iCloud Backup or computer backup if you have that turned on; that backup is yours, under your Apple Account.

Card photos. If you add a photo to an account, it's saved on that device only. It isn't synced or uploaded. The app asks you to cover the card number before taking one, and it never asks for or stores full card numbers. The optional "last 4 digits" field is kept with the account.

Backups. If you export a backup file, it goes wherever you choose to save or send it.

iCloud sync (optional, CalcOwlator Pro)

When you turn on iCloud sync, your lists, groups and accounts are stored in the private database of your iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit. Apple stores this data. We, the developer, cannot see or access it. You can turn sync off in the app's Settings, and remove the data in your iPhone's Settings › [your name] › iCloud.

Purchases

CalcOwlator Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment. We receive no payment details, only whether your Apple ID has Pro, which the app checks on your device.

Face ID and passcode

If you turn on the lock, iOS checks your face, fingerprint or passcode. The app only learns whether the check succeeded. It never sees or stores biometric data.

Notifications

Bill and card reminders are created on your device from your own lists and due dates. They're not sent through any server.

Camera and photos

When you scan a receipt, bill, barcode or QR code, or pick a photo, the text and codes are read on your iPhone by Apple's built-in recognition. Images aren't saved by the app and are never uploaded. Names and prices you give barcodes, and the sections the app learns for items, stay on your device.

Widgets

Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets show a summary the app saves on your device for them. With the lock turned on, widgets show no amounts.

What we don't do

  • No analytics or crash-reporting services are built into the app.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No connection to your bank. Account names, colours and balances are only what you type in.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of anything.

If you choose to share crash reports with developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may pass anonymous crash data to us. This is controlled by iOS, not the app.

Children

CalcOwlator doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. If the app ever starts collecting data, we'll say so here and in the app before it does.
