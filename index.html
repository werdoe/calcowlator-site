Every row adds up.

CalcOwlator is a list calculator for iPhone. Budgets, bills, groceries, trips and savings goals: name each row, give it an amount, and the totals work themselves out.

What it does

  • Sections and totals that stay right when you change anything
  • Start next month's budget from the menu, with what's left carried over
  • Send money to a savings goal from your budget
  • Accounts and cards: what comes out of where, statements and payment reminders
  • Your lists stay on your iPhone, with optional iCloud sync
