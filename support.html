Common questions

Where are my lists kept?

On your iPhone. With CalcOwlator Pro you can also turn on iCloud sync in Settings to keep them on all your devices and safe when you change phones. You can also export a backup file from Settings at any time.

How do I get Pro back on a new phone?

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought it with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions › CalcOwlator Pro. Cancelling keeps Pro until the end of the period you've paid for. Nothing you made is ever locked.

Why doesn't an account's balance change?

Balances follow rows once they're ticked, meaning the money has actually moved. Open the account to see what's still planned and tap Done, or tick the row in its list.

I'm not getting reminders.

Check that notifications are allowed for CalcOwlator in your iPhone's Settings, that Reminders is on in the app's Settings, and that the row has a due date (or the card has a payment day).

Does CalcOwlator connect to my bank?

No. Accounts are only what you type in, which keeps everything private and on your phone.
